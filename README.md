# Neon Sunset Studio

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-cover-art-01.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01)
[![rank](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-cover-art-01/usb-star001/gta6-cover-art-01/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01)
[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-cover-art-01/usb-star001/gta6-cover-art-01/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01)

An offline web app that turns your photos and a caption into GTA-style cover art: a multi-panel poster with bold caption lettering and a Vice City sunset or neon colour grade, exported as a PNG to share.

- **Your photos** — upload up to 3, one per panel, or use the built-in procedurally drawn sunset scenes. Click a panel to pick its photo, zoom, and pan (or drag on the preview).
- **Three layouts** — Triptych (3 tall panels), Hero (big top, two below), Quad (2×2).
- **Colour grades** — Sunset, Neon, Midnight, or none.
- **Fast** — a photo and a caption become a cover in a few clicks, exported at 1080×1080, 1080×1350, or 1920×1080.

Everything runs offline from static files: open `app/index.html` in a desktop browser, no network needed. All artwork is drawn by the app itself — no borrowed images, no game assets.

Built for the [make.fun GTA 6 Cover Art bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01).
